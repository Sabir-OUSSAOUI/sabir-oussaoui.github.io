About Me

I am a PhD researcher specializing in Climate Change, Remote Sensing, and Artificial Intelligence at the University Mohammed 6 Polytechnic (UM6P) and Québec Trois-Rivières University (UQTR) through a cotutelle program.

My research focuses on drought monitoring and forecasting in Morocco using advanced remote sensing techniques and socioeconomic data. I combine satellite imagery analysis, machine learning approaches, and field surveys to develop innovative solutions for agricultural resilience and water sustainability.

With extensive experience in GIS, remote sensing, and environmental geomatics, I have contributed to multiple high-impact publications in Q1 journals and presented at international conferences. I am passionate about leveraging technology to address climate challenges and support sustainable development.
